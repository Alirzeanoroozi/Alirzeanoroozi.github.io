Graduate Researcher — antimicrobial peptide design

Koç University · Oct 2025 – Present

  • AMP-DiT: a classifier-conditioned discrete diffusion transformer that generates antimicrobial peptides directly in sequence space, avoiding the representation mismatch of protein language models trained on full-length proteins. Accepted as a poster at the GenBio Workshop at ICML 2026 and submitted to the AMP Challenge
  • AMP-BindGen: guided design of peptide binders and inhibitors against the carbapenemases NDM-5 and KPC-3, with a composite ranking scheme over interface confidence, catalytic-site engagement, predicted activity and toxicity. Designs have been validated experimentally in the lab
  • TED: an end-to-end sequence-to-sequence transformer predicting protein domain boundaries and CATH identifiers directly from sequence, trained on ~186M sequences with LoRA fine-tuning over ESM-2 embeddings
  • BoltzGen-ADC: generative design of protease-cleavable peptide linkers for antibody–drug conjugates, submitted to the Merck Challenge (with Mahda Ghasemiagreh)
  • Collaboration with KUISCID and the School of Medicine on carbapenem resistance
